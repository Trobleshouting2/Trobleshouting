My Hobbies

Chess

Chess is one of my favorite games because it challenges me to think carefully and plan my moves. It is a strategy game played between two players on a board with 64 squares and 16 pieces for each player.

I enjoy chess because it helps me improve my concentration, patience, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. Every game is different, and I need to think ahead, understand my opponent's plans, and find the best moves.

Chess also teaches me that every decision has consequences. I like learning new strategies, improving my skills, and challenging myself to become a better player.

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) is one of my favorite mobile games. It is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game where two teams compete against each other. Players choose different heroes with unique abilities and roles.

I enjoy learning about different heroes, practicing their skills, developing strategies, and working with my teammates. Teamwork, communication, map awareness, and good decision-making are important for winning matches.

My Favorite Hero: Thamuz

My favorite hero in MLBB is Thamuz, the Lord Lava. He is a Fighter hero known for his aggressive fighting style, strong close-range attacks, and ability to deal sustained damage in battles.

I like Thamuz because his skills allow him to pressure opponents and stay active during team fights. His abilities make him enjoyable to use when fighting enemies and helping my team. Learning when to attack, when to retreat, and how to use his skills effectively makes playing him more interesting.

Playing MLBB is entertaining, and it also gives me opportunities to practice teamwork, strategy, and quick decision-making.
