🌿 NUTRITION MONTH 🌿

Eat Healthy, Live Healthy, Grow Strong!

Nutrition Month is celebrated every July in the Philippines to encourage everyone to eat healthy foods and live an active lifestyle. It reminds people that good nutrition helps us stay strong, healthy, and ready to learn every day.

🥗 Why Healthy Eating Is Important

Eating a balanced diet gives our body the energy it needs. Fruits and vegetables provide vitamins and minerals that help protect us from diseases.

Drinking enough water, exercising regularly, and avoiding too much junk food can help improve our overall health and well-being.

🍎 Healthy Food Examples

  • 🍎 Apples
  • 🍌 Bananas
  • 🥕 Carrots
  • 🥬 Green Leafy Vegetables
  • 🥛 Milk
  • 🐟 Fish
