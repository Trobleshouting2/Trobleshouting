Chess

My Favorite Sports Game | Quarter 2 - Activity 1

What is Chess?

Chess is a strategy board game played by two players. Each player tries to checkmate the opponent's king. The game requires planning, concentration, and logical thinking.

Why I Like Chess

I like chess because it challenges my mind and helps me improve my problem-solving and decision-making skills. Every game can be different because players can use different strategies.

The Chess Pieces

  • King - The most important piece that must be protected.
  • Queen - The most powerful piece and can move in many directions.
  • Rook - Moves horizontally and vertically.
  • Bishop - Moves diagonally.
  • Knight - Moves in an L-shaped pattern and can jump over pieces.
  • Pawn - Usually moves forward and captures diagonally.

Basic Chess Rules

  • Players take turns moving one piece at a time.
  • A player cannot make a move that leaves their own king in check.
  • The game ends when a king is checkmated.
  • A game can also end in a draw.

Important Chess Skills

  • Planning ahead
  • Concentration
  • Problem-solving
  • Patience
  • Strategic thinking

My Conclusion

Chess is more than just a board game. It is a fun way to develop critical thinking, patience, and strategic skills.
