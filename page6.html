My Favorite Sports Game: Chess

Chess is my favorite sports game because it improves my thinking, planning, and problem-solving skills.

I enjoy playing chess because every game is different and requires careful decisions.

About Chess

Chess is a strategy board game played between two players. Each player starts with 16 pieces, including a king, queen, rooks, bishops, knights, and pawns.

The goal of chess is to checkmate the opponent's king. Players must think carefully and plan their moves to gain an advantage.

Why I Like Chess

I like chess because it is challenging and fun. It helps me practice patience, concentration, strategy, and decision-making.
